About this list

What makes a game my favorite?

This site is a personal shortlist of games that are memorable for different reasons, not a definitive ranking of the medium.

The idea

A list with a little variety

The five picks cover different kinds of play: building, simulation, exploration, puzzles, and racing. The order reflects personal preference, while the short reviews explain what makes each game stand out.

How to explore

Start anywhere

Visit the rankings page for the complete ordered list, or head to reviews for a closer look at each game. The homepage search can also narrow the list by title or genre.

A note on the picks

Make this list yours

The titles and descriptions are starter examples. Replace them with your own favorite games and personal reasons for choosing them.
