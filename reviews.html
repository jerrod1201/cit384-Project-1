A closer look

Why these games stay with me.

Each pick brings something different to the table. Here are a few details that make each one worth returning to.

01 · Sandbox

Build and explore

Minecraft

A game that makes creativity part of the adventure.

The block-based world is simple to understand and surprisingly flexible. Gathering resources, building a base, or wandering into the unknown can each become the whole point of a session.

02 · Simulation

Slow down and grow

Stardew Valley

A warm routine with plenty of room for surprises.

Planning a day around crops and errands gives the game a satisfying pace. New areas, characters, and seasonal events keep the routine from feeling too familiar.

03 · Adventure

Follow your curiosity

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild

A wide-open adventure that rewards looking around.

Instead of pushing players along one route, the landscape is full of landmarks that invite investigation. Trying an unusual approach is often just as satisfying as finding the intended solution.

04 · Puzzle

Think in portals

Portal 2

A smart puzzle game with a clear, memorable central mechanic.

Each test chamber builds on the portal tool in a way that encourages players to observe, experiment, and rethink the room. Its humor gives the puzzles a distinct personality.

05 · Racing

One more round

Mario Kart 8 Deluxe

Quick races that are easy to pick up and fun to replay.

Distinct tracks, responsive controls, and a mix of familiar characters make each cup feel lively. The unpredictable items can turn even a close race into a dramatic finish.
